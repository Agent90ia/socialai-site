Social AI YouTube Connector

A private publishing tool of the Social AI project. With the channel owner's Google authorization, it reads basic information about our YouTube channels and uploads the videos we produce to them.

What the connector does

Social AI YouTube Connector connects the Social AI editorial workflow to YouTube through the official YouTube Data API. It is used to manage three YouTube channels run by the Social AI project: Ninth Clause, Banco di Lavoro and GantryGrid.

1. Google authorization

The owner of a channel signs in with Google and explicitly grants access on Google's own consent screen. The connector never sees or stores Google passwords.

2. Read channel information

It reads channel details such as the channel name and ID and the list and status of videos already uploaded, so the team knows what is published.

3. Upload videos

It uploads videos prepared by the team to the authorized channel, with the title, description and privacy setting chosen by the channel owner.

4. Analytics (in integration)

Being integrated, not yet operational: reading YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, engagement) to improve the channels' editorial strategy.

Who can use it

The connector is an internal tool. It is not offered as a public service and there is no sign-up for the general public: only the owners and managers of the Social AI channels listed above authorize it.

Your data and your control

Google user data is used only to provide these functions, is never sold and is not used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account permissions page. Full details are in the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.

Social AI YouTube Connector uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
