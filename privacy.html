Privacy Policy

Social AI YouTube Connector · Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Social AI YouTube Connector (the "Connector") accesses, collects, stores, uses and shares when a user authorizes it with a Google Account, and how users can control and delete that information. By authorizing the Connector you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Who we are

The Connector is an internal publishing tool of the Social AI project, used to manage the YouTube channels Ninth Clause, Banco di Lavoro and GantryGrid. Contact for any privacy question, request or complaint: socialai.editorial@gmail.com.

2. Use of YouTube API Services and Google APIs

The Connector uses YouTube API Services: the YouTube Data API v3 and, once its integration is completed, the YouTube Analytics API. Information you make available to Google is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

Social AI YouTube Connector's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Google user data we access

The Connector only accesses data after the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission on Google's consent screen. It requests the following three permissions:

Permission (OAuth scope)What it allowsWhy we need it
View your YouTube account
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly		Read channel details (channel ID, title, description, thumbnails), the channel's video list, video metadata, processing and privacy status, and public statistics such as view counts.To identify the authorized channel and check that uploads were received and processed correctly.
Manage your YouTube videos
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload		Upload videos and set their metadata (title, description, tags, category, privacy status).To publish the videos prepared by the Social AI team to the authorized channel.
View YouTube Analytics reports for your YouTube content
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly		Read YouTube Analytics reports for the authorized channel and its videos, such as views, watch time, average view duration and engagement metrics (for example likes, comments and shares counts).To analyse the performance of the published videos and improve the editorial strategy of the channels.

Status of the Analytics access. The YouTube Analytics permission has been added to the Connector's Google project, but the analytics function is still being integrated and is not yet operational. Until it is in operation, the Connector does not use this permission to retrieve analytics data. This policy describes how the data will be handled once the function is active.

The Connector also receives from Google an OAuth access token and refresh token, which let it act on the channel within the permissions above. The Connector never asks for, receives or stores your Google or YouTube password.

The Connector does not read the content of YouTube comments, private messages or subscriber lists, does not access payment or revenue data, and does not access any Google service other than YouTube.

4. How we use the data

  • to upload videos to the authorized channel, with the metadata decided by the channel owner;
  • to read the channel's information and video status so the editorial team can confirm what has been published;
  • once the analytics function is operational: to analyse aggregated performance metrics of the channels and their videos (views, watch time, engagement) in order to plan and improve future content;
  • to keep operational logs (date, operation, outcome, video ID) needed for reliability, security and troubleshooting.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not use it to build user profiles, and we do not use it to develop, improve or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models. Data is used only for the purposes described here.

The editorial analysis may be supported by AI tools. If channel or analytics data is processed with an AI service for this purpose, it is used only to produce the analysis requested by the Social AI team for the authorized channels, it is not used to train or improve generalized AI models, and it is shared only with providers listed in section 5.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with third parties. Data is transferred only:

  • to Google/YouTube, when the Connector sends a video and its metadata at the channel owner's request;
  • to infrastructure providers that host the Connector, strictly to run it;
  • to AI service providers, only if and when used for the editorial analysis described in section 4, only with the data needed for that analysis, and only under terms that do not allow them to use it to train their models;
  • when required by law or to protect the security of users or of the service.

The Connector does not allow third parties to serve content or advertisements.

6. Storage, retention and deletion

We keep Google user data only as long as needed for the purposes in section 4, within the limits set by the YouTube API Services Developer Policies:

  • Authorization tokens are kept on infrastructure used by the Social AI project, with access restricted to its administrators, only while the authorization is active.
  • YouTube data obtained through the API (channel and video information, statistics and, once active, analytics reports) is not kept for more than 30 calendar days: after that period it is deleted or refreshed with current data from the API.
  • Operational logs contain only technical information (date, operation, outcome, video ID) and are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting.
  • Video files prepared for upload are created by the Social AI team; they are not YouTube data.
  • After revocation (see section 7), we stop using the authorization and delete the YouTube data obtained under it within 7 calendar days.

7. How to revoke access and request deletion

You can revoke the Connector's access at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Revoking access there immediately stops the Connector from using your authorization. You can also ask us to revoke the authorization and delete your data by writing to socialai.editorial@gmail.com: we will revoke the token with Google and delete the related data within 7 calendar days of the request, and confirm by email.

8. Security

We protect data by restricting access to authorized administrators, by communicating with Google APIs only over HTTPS, and by requesting only the permissions needed for the functions described above. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect the data against unauthorized access, loss and misuse.

9. Cookies and this website

This website (socialai.rinveta.com) does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts and does not store information on your device. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as IP addresses in its server logs for security purposes. The Connector itself is not a browser application and does not place cookies on users' devices.

10. Your rights

Where data protection law (such as the EU GDPR) applies, you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, restriction of or objection to processing, and data portability, and you can lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Write to socialai.editorial@gmail.com.

11. Children

The Connector is an internal tool not directed at children and is not used by people under 18.

12. Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and will show the new date at the top. Material changes affecting Google user data will be applied only with any new consent required by Google.
